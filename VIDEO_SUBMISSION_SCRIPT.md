---

### Scene 2: The Problem (0:25 – 0:55)
**Visual on Screen:**  
- B-roll / animations showing messy multi-cloud platforms (Windows Active Directory, Linux Servers, GitHub, AWS).  
- Graphic illustrating an employee switching roles or leaving, but their admin access remains active.

**Voiceover / Presenter:**
> *"In large organizations with thousands of employees and multi-cloud environments, employee roles change constantly. When someone moves departments or leaves the company, their old permissions are rarely revoked. This is known as **'Access Creep'**.*  
> *Dormant accounts, orphaned credentials, and excessive admin privileges create massive security blind spots, making companies vulnerable to data breaches and compliance failures.*  
> *Traditional manual audits are slow, error-prone, and overwhelm security teams with thousands of spreadsheets."*

---

### Scene 3: The Solution — AccessGuard (0:55 – 1:45)
**Visual on Screen:**  
- Screen recording of the **AccessGuard Dark-Themed Dashboard**.
- Show the **Permission Matrix**, **Risk Scores (Rule-based & ML percentiles)**, and **Review Campaigns**.
- Quick clip showing Dialog Access Log integration and automated anomaly alerts.

**Voiceover / Presenter:**
> *"To solve this, we developed **AccessGuard** — an intelligent, centralized Access Governance and Administration platform.*  
> *Here is how it works:*  
> 1. ***Unified Identity Mapping:*** *AccessGuard automatically consolidates accounts across Active Directory, Linux, and GitHub into single, verifiable human identities.*  
> 2. ***Explainable Risk Engine:*** *It evaluates access risks in real time, assigning transparent risk scores based on excessive permissions, cross-department access, and inactivity.*  
> 3. ***AI Anomaly Detection:*** *Powered by Unsupervised Machine Learning (Isolation Forest), it spots subtle behavioral anomalies and privilege escalations that human auditors would easily miss.*  
> 4. ***Human-in-the-Loop Reviews:*** *Managers can review, certify, or simulate access revocations with a single click, keeping audits frictionless and secure."*

## 💡 Quick Tips for Recording

1. **Audio Quality:** Use a good microphone or hands-free headset in a quiet room — clear audio is more important than camera 4K resolution.
2. **Screen Clarity:** When showing the AccessGuard dashboard, zoom your browser to 110%–125% so table texts and risk badges are easy to read on a large ceremony projector.
3. **Pacing:** Keep your speech calm, energetic, and under 2 minutes and 30 seconds to stay safely within the 1–3 minute limit.
4. **Subtitles:** Add burned-in English captions (subtitles) if possible, so guests in a noisy hall can follow easily.
